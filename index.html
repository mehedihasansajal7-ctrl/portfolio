ERP · Trading

Integrated Sales, Order, Inventory & Delivery ERP

Bulk-Commodity Trading · Sales ERP

ERP for a bulk-commodity trading business (fertilizer, coal, grain) — quantities in MT, money in BDT, every date boundary evaluated in Asia/Dhaka. Five job functions plus admin work on one database, each seeing only what its role allows. The rules are enforced in the code and in the database itself: Finance’s rate is copied into an order and locked permanently, per-customer delivery priority is enforced across products inside a row-locked transaction, over-allocation and over-dispatch are rejected by CHECK constraints, stock is an immutable ledger, and the audit log is append-only (a database trigger refuses UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE). 12 reports with Excel / PDF / CSV export, plus printables for the order confirmation, delivery order and dispatch challan.
