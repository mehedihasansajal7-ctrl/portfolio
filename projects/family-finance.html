About this project

A private family finance manager for Android, with an installable web PWA on the same Firebase project. Every family member signs in with their own account and keeps their own financial records; the family shares one dashboard that shows aggregate totals only.

The central rule is that individual financial information is private — not another member, not an admin, not the family owner can read your income, balances, bank or bKash/Nagad numbers, loans or savings. That is enforced in the database rules, not by hiding buttons.

What it does

  • Per-member private ledger: accounts (bank, bKash, Nagad, card, DPS, FDR), income, expenses, transfers, goals, loans, receipts.
  • Family dashboard with aggregate income, expense, savings, budgets (75% / 90% / 100% alerts), goals, bills and PDF reports.
  • Offline-first: every write commits locally first and syncs later, with no possibility of double-posting.
  • Bengali numerals with South-Asian lakh/crore grouping, light and dark themes, biometric app lock.
  • Transfers between your own accounts never distort income or expense totals.
