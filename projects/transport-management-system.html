About this project

A role-based company transport system for three very different users: an admin who runs the fleet, an employee who needs a car, and a driver on a phone who needs today's trips.

It covers the whole lifecycle — request → approval → assignment → trip — and enforces the rules on the server, so a double-booking or an over-capacity assignment is impossible even through the API.

What it does

  • Capacity is checked against the vehicle; a smaller vehicle is never auto-assigned, and a larger one needs a written reason.
  • Time-range conflict detection with a transactional re-check, so two simultaneous approvals cannot double-book a car.
  • Cancellations release the vehicle and driver without deleting history; material edits after approval send the request back for re-approval.
  • Driver availability is two separate concepts: operational status and trip state, with AWAITING_CONFIRMATION after every trip.
  • Bulk Excel/CSV onboarding, notifications, reports and an immutable audit trail.
  • Passenger phone numbers are never collected, so they cannot leak to drivers.
