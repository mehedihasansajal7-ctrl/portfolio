About this project

A self-hosted control tower for the trust. The transaction register is fed live from the ERP data warehouse (SQL Server), with month-wise payment-by-category analytics, financial-year reporting and an hourly auto-refresh.

The same dashboard hosts a field-visit tracker: visit planning, GPS-verified check-in, photo evidence, structured reports, supervisor approval and follow-up tasks — with no third-party dependencies at all.

What it does

  • Live ERP register with a one-hour cache and an hourly client refresh; a silent fallback would be visible, not hidden.
  • Month-wise payment by category, financial-year report and CSV exports.
  • GPS check-in verified server-side with the Haversine formula against a configurable radius and accuracy threshold.
  • Up to ten photos per visit, validated by magic bytes and stored unmodified.
  • Supervisor approve / return-for-correction, with approved reports becoming read-only.
  • Append-only audit log and eleven CSV exports across plans, visits, GPS, follow-ups and email log.
