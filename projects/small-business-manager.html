About this project

An offline-first business and farm management system for a small business owned by two to five partners in Bangladesh. Every partner signs in on their own device and shares the same business data according to their role — with zero mandatory cost: no paid hosting, no paid AI, no card.

The same app carries a complete farming module, because the shop and the farm are the same business: a harvest becomes sellable stock, and its cost has to trace back to the cycle that produced it.

What it does

  • POS sales, purchases and supplier bills, payables, receivables, partner capital and drawings.
  • Inventory as an append-only ledger with weighted-average valuation, transfers, counts and reason-coded adjustments.
  • Double-entry journal with trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow and stock valuation reports.
  • Farming: farms → units → production cycles (crop, greenhouse, livestock, poultry, aquaculture) with input issues, mortality, harvests and cycle profitability.
  • Shared-cost allocation across cycles with nothing lost to rounding.
  • A bilingual Bangla/English AI assistant that explains and suggests but never posts anything.
  • Local mode needs no account and no network; the phone app and web PWA share one dataset.
